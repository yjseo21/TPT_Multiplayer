# TPT multiplayer: joining from the game, player list, edit preview (P3b)

**Engineering handoff, 2 November 2026.** Second slice of phase P3 of
`IN_GAME_MULTIPLAYER_PLAN.md`, on top of P3a (`IN_GAME_P3A_2026-11-02.md`).

## What works

- **Ctrl+Shift+J** opens the room dialog. It has fields for:
  - the address (`host:port` means `wss://host:port`; a plain `ws://` address
    works only for this computer);
  - your name (it defaults to your powdertoy.co.uk username);
  - the room password;
  - an admin key.

  The password fields are masked. In a room, the dialog also offers
  **Leave room** and **Join instead**. **Ctrl+Shift+M** opens the dialog when
  you are not in a room. Command-line `join:` / `name:` and the
  `TPT_ROOM_TOKEN` / `TPT_ADMIN_TOKEN` variables still work.
- The address and name are saved in the game's preferences
  (`Multiplayer.Room`, `Multiplayer.Name`). Passwords are kept in memory for
  the session and never written to disk.
- **Player list:** a line under the room status names everyone in the room and
  marks you and the admins.
- **Other players' cursors:** each one is drawn as its brush outline with the
  player's name, filled in while they are drawing. Each player gets their own
  colour.
- **Edit preview:** your own strokes appear immediately as a translucent
  overlay in the tool's colour, until the room applies them to your local
  world. Whatever the network delay, a stroke shows where it will land at once.
  - It covers both edits waiting for the room and edits still queued by the
    pacing outbox, so a huge stroke being paced in shows in full at once.
  - It follows the brush shape (all four shapes), the X/Y radius, rectangles,
    and wall cells.
  - Fills and simulation tools (heat, air and so on) are not previewed: their
    effect is only known once applied.
  - The preview is render-only and never touches physics, so it cannot affect
    determinism. Rejected edits drop out of it after 3 s.
  - Each frame does at most 4M preview writes, so a huge brush never slows a
    frame.
- **The room keeps running under dialogs.** The engine has a background tick,
  so a window over the game no longer freezes the room:
  - affected windows include this dialog, the save browser, options, the
    element search and the Lua console;
  - before, the local world stopped while one was open. On closing it, the
    client tried to catch up the whole backlog in one frame, a freeze of
    several seconds after a long visit.

## Tests

`multiplayer-game-room` (real `powder`, all four CI platforms) now also covers:
- opening the dialog over a running room;
- checking that the room's local tick keeps advancing underneath;
- closing the dialog with Escape;
- leaving, then rejoining through the dialog with Enter. The address comes
  from saved preferences, and the rejoined connection must synchronize
  without a resync.

It also asserts the player list (`Game (you, admin)`, `Watcher`), that pending
edits were previewed, and that the address was saved. Edit agreement with the
watcher is checked at a mark taken just before leaving.

Visual check (automation `capture` step, which saves the window as a PNG):
- the preview of a radius-45 water stroke while the stroke is in flight;
- the applied result;
- the dialog over the running room.

All were checked on Linux.

New automation steps: `leave`, `dialog`, `dialogjoin`, `dialogcancel`, `mark`
and `capture`.
